Aircraft Design — Amphibious Aircraft

A computational framework for analysing and optimising amphibious aircraft designs with hydrofoils.

Jan 10, 2021

A computational framework for analysing and optimising amphibious aircraft designs.

  • Developed a conceptual design framework for amphibious aircraft with hydrofoils.
  • Developed surrogate models for multiphase CFD analyses of supercavitating hydrofoils and hulls.
  • Optimised hydrofoil geometry for improved takeoff performance with these models.

Multiphase CFD sweeps over speed and angle of attack produce surrogate models for the hydrodynamic coefficients of a supercavitating hydrofoil, capturing the water volume fraction around the wetted section.

These surrogates feed a water-takeoff analysis coupling hydrofoil, hull, and aerodynamic loads, over which the hydrofoil incidence and span are optimised to minimise takeoff distance.

Publications

  • James M. Shihua, Arjit Seth, Ye Li, and Rhea P. Liem. “Experimental and Computational Analyses of Take-off Hydrodynamics of an Amphibian Aircraft Hull”. AIAA AVIATION 2020 Forum, 2020, p. 3174. doi:10.2514/6.2020-3174
  • Arjit Seth and Rhea P. Liem. “Amphibious Aircraft Developments: Computational Studies of Hydrofoil Design for Improvements in Water-Takeoffs”. Aerospace 8.1 (2021), p. 10. doi:10.3390/aerospace8010010

This work formed the basis of my MPhil thesis, A Computational Design Framework for Amphibious Aircraft.
